Our Heritage & Origin

From Mount Taygetos to the Gates of Hades

Mani is situated in South Greece, stretching from the majestic mountain peaks of Taygetos down to Cape Tenaro — known in antiquity as the legendary Gate of Hades — the southernmost point of continental Europe.

Typical characteristics of Mani’s landscape are its imposing stone towers, arid rocky soil, wild natural thyme herbs, and ancient olive trees rooted deep into the Mediterranean limestone. The untamed nature, fierce sun, and dry coastal winds compose the perfect, inimitable recipe for world-class olive oil.

The Koutrakos family knows this sacred secret intimately. Passed down with unwavering respect from generation to generation, strict traditional harvesting and extraction methods are preserved to ensure that every single drop of Mani's Secret reflects the soul of this historic land.

"We don't merely produce olive oil; we preserve a living centuries-old heritage of health, longevity, and pure Mediterranean nutrition."

— The Koutrakos Family, Mani
